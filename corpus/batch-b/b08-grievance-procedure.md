# Student Grievance Procedure

> Synthetic test document for RAHBAR (CS4032 Assignment 03, Batch B). Not an official FAST-NUCES publication.

A student who has a complaint about a grade, a teacher or an administrative decision first discusses it with the course instructor or the office concerned within seven days. If the matter is not settled, the student submits a written grievance to the Head of Department, who replies within ten working days.

An appeal against the Head of Department's decision goes to the Grievance Committee, which hears the student and the staff member separately and gives a final decision within one month. A grievance is never held against the student who raised it.
