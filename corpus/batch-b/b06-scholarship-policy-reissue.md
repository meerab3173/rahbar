# Scholarship Policy (merit and need based)

> Synthetic test document for RAHBAR (CS4032 Assignment 03, Batch B). Not an official FAST-NUCES publication.

## 1. Merit scholarships

A student who is in the top five percent of a programme at the end of the academic year receives a merit scholarship covering fifty percent of the tuition fee for the next year. The scholarship continues only while the student keeps a semester GPA of at least 3.50 and takes a full course load.

## 2. Need-based financial assistance

A student who cannot pay full tuition may apply for need-based assistance in the first four weeks of the semester. The application includes the family income certificate and the latest utility bills. A committee interviews each applicant and may cover up to one hundred percent of the tuition.

## 3. Losing a scholarship

The scholarship is withdrawn if the student is placed on academic probation, is found guilty of a disciplinary offence, or gave false information in the application. A withdrawn scholarship may be applied for again after one semester.

## 4. How to apply

Applications are submitted on the student portal. Late applications are not considered for the current semester.
