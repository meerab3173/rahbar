# Library Borrowing Rules

> Synthetic test document for RAHBAR (CS4032 Assignment 03, Batch B). Not an official FAST-NUCES publication.

## 1. Who may borrow

Every enrolled undergraduate student may borrow up to three books at a time on the university identity card. Graduate students may borrow up to five books. Reference books, theses and journals are for reading inside the library and are never issued.

## 2. Loan period and renewal

Books are issued for fourteen days. A loan can be renewed twice at the circulation desk or through the library portal, provided no other student has reserved the book.

## 3. Late returns and lost books

A book returned late is charged a fine of twenty rupees per day. A student who loses a book pays its current price plus a processing fee of five hundred rupees. Library dues must be cleared before the final transcript is issued.

## 4. Conduct in the library

Silence is kept in the reading halls. Food and drinks are not allowed, and group discussion is allowed only in the discussion rooms on the first floor.
