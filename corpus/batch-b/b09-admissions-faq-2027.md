# Admissions FAQ (2027 intake)

> Synthetic test document for RAHBAR (CS4032 Assignment 03, Batch B). Not an official FAST-NUCES publication.

## Can I apply to more than one campus?

Yes. One application form lets you choose up to three campuses in order of preference. The merit list is prepared separately for each campus.

## Is the admission test the same for every programme?

No. Engineering and computing programmes use the university's own admission test. Business programmes accept either the university's test or an approved external test score.

## When are the merit lists announced?

The first merit list for the 2027 intake is announced in the first week of August, and later lists follow weekly until all seats are filled.

## Can I change my programme after admission?

A change of programme is allowed only in the first two weeks of the first semester, and only if the student meets the merit of the new programme.
