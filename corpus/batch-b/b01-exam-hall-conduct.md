# Examination Hall Conduct Notice

> Synthetic test document for RAHBAR (CS4032 Assignment 03, Batch B). Not an official FAST-NUCES publication.

## 1. Entry to the examination hall

Students must reach the examination hall fifteen minutes before the paper starts. A student who arrives more than thirty minutes late is not allowed to enter and is marked absent for that paper. The university identity card must be shown to the invigilator at the door and kept on the desk for the whole paper.

## 2. Items that are not allowed

Mobile phones, smart watches, earphones and programmable calculators are not allowed in the hall, even when they are switched off. Bags and books are left at the front of the hall before the paper starts. A student found with a prohibited item is reported to the Disciplinary Committee, whatever the item was used for.

## 3. Leaving the hall

No student may leave the hall during the first hour of a paper or during its last fifteen minutes. A student who needs the washroom is escorted by an invigilator, and the time out of the hall is recorded on the attendance sheet.

## 4. Unfair means

Copying from another student, talking during the paper and passing material are treated as unfair means. The invigilator takes the answer book, records the time and the reason, and forwards the case to the Disciplinary Committee, which decides the penalty under the student handbook.
